# R-C09 Architecture and roadmap
Architecture: tabs Songs | Alphabets | Review | You. Feature modules songs, alphabets, review, paywall. Services tokenizer, romaji, dictionary, translator, TTS, SRS, streak, notifications, purchases. SQLite/Drizzle candidate; bundled read-only dictionary DB. Optional adapters: G1-gated lyric provider, YouTube player, local audio, cloud sync.
Core concepts: Song(id,title,artist,language,source,audioRef,syncOffsetMs); Line(id,startMs,text,tokens,translation); Token(surface,reading,romaji,base,pos,glossId); Card(id,kind,refId,FSRS state); ReviewLog(id,cardId,timestamp,rating,elapsedMs,mode).
