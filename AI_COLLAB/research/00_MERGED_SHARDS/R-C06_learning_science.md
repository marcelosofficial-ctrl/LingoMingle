# R-C06 Learning science
Use retrieval practice: ask for recall before revealing answer. Space reviews over time and adapt to recall difficulty; FSRS is a candidate scheduler. Give prompt, specific feedback and retry. Interleave confusable items only after basic familiarity. Pair glyph, sound and distinctive original image mnemonic; mnemonics are hooks, not substitutes for retrieval. Pair sound with orthography and gradually fade romanisation. Use sentence/song/dialogue context alongside token gloss. Vary retrieval direction: glyph->sound, sound->glyph, audio->glyph, typing, matching and confusable discrimination.
Product rules: small groups; adjustable daily new-card load; mix due cards and limited new items; streaks reward consistency without shame; rest/repair options; hints fade gradually; separate learning from review; mastery estimates are estimates. Test mnemonic quality with real beginners; resemblance is not universal.
